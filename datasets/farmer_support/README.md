# Crop insurance & agricultural credit schemes for Bangladesh

This directory holds the crop-insurance and agricultural-credit reference
data used by the "Farmer Support" feature of the platform.

## Sources

1. Bangladesh Bank — "Agricultural Credit Programmes" annual report and
   "Refinance Scheme for Agricultural Development".
2. Bangladesh Krishi Rin Jata Samiti / Kisan Credit Card (KCC) scheme.
3. Pragati Insurance / Krishi Bima Tatha Punonayan — crop insurance
   products.

## Schemes covered

| Scheme | Type | Who qualifies | Key terms |
|---|---|---|---|
| Kisan Credit Card (KCC) | Low-interest credit | All registered farmers | 4% interest, revolving limit |
| Agricultural Credit Programme | Production credit | Small & marginal farmers | Co-operative / bank disbursement |
| Crop Insurance (Krishi Bima) | Risk cover | Farmers of insured crops | Premium shared govt:farmer 50:50 |
| Livestock / Fisheries Credit | Sector credit | Livestock / fish farmers | Refinance at 4-5% |

Last verified: 2026-09-06. Source: Bangladesh Bank agricultural credit
statistics (publicly documented annual figures).
