# Dhaka District

## Overview
Dhaka is the capital and largest city of Bangladesh, located in the central region of the country. It serves as the political, economic, and cultural hub of Bangladesh with a population of over 22 million in the greater metropolitan area.

## Key Attractions

### Historical Sites
- **Lalbagh Fort** — 17th century Mughal fort complex with beautiful gardens and museum
- **Ahsan Manzil** — Pink Palace, former residence of the Nawabs of Dhaka
- **Star Mosque (Tara Masjid)** — 19th century mosque decorated with mosaic stars
- **Armenian Church** — 1781 Armenian Apostolic Church in Armanitola
- **Dhakeshwari Temple** — National Hindu temple of Bangladesh

### Museums
- **Bangladesh National Museum** — Largest museum in Bangladesh with extensive collections
- **Liberation War Museum** — Documents the 1971 Bangladesh Liberation War
- **Bangabandhu Memorial Museum** — Former residence of Sheikh Mujibur Rahman

### Parks & Recreation
- **Ramna Park** — Large urban park with lake and walking paths
- **Baldha Garden** — Botanical garden with rare plant species
- **National Botanical Garden** — 205-acre garden in Mirpur

## Transportation
- **Hazrat Shahjalal International Airport (DAC)** — Main international gateway
- **Kamalapur Railway Station** — Central railway hub
- **Sayedabad Bus Terminal** — Major inter-district bus terminal
- **Metro Rail (MRT Line-6)** — Uttara to Motijheel (operational)
- **BRTC & Private Buses** — Extensive city bus network
- **Ride-sharing** — Uber, Pathao, Shohoz available

## Accommodation Areas
- **Gulshan/Banani** — Diplomatic zone, luxury hotels, expat area
- **Dhanmondi** — Residential, mid-range hotels, restaurants
- **Motijheel** — Business district, budget hotels
- **Uttara** — Near airport, growing hotel options
- **Old Dhaka** — Heritage hotels, budget options

## Food Specialties
- **Kacchi Biryani** — Mutton biryani with potatoes (Old Dhaka specialty)
- **Morog Polao** — Chicken pilaf
- **Bakarkhani** — Traditional flatbread
- **Haji Biryani** — Famous biryani chain
- **Street food** — Fuchka, chotpoti, jhalmuri in Chawkbazar

## Best Time to Visit
October to March (cool, dry season). Avoid April-June (extreme heat) and July-September (monsoon).

## Estimated Costs (per day, per person)
- Budget: ৳2,000-3,500
- Mid-range: ৳5,000-10,000
- Luxury: ৳15,000+

## Language
Bengali (primary), English (widely understood in business/tourism areas)

## Safety Tips
- Use ride-sharing apps for transport
- Avoid Old Dhaka at night without guide
- Keep valuables secure in crowded areas
- Drink bottled water only
