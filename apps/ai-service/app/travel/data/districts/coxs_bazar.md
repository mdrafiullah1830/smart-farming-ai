# Cox's Bazar District

## Overview
Cox's Bazar is a coastal town in southeastern Bangladesh, famous for the world's longest natural sandy sea beach (120 km unbroken). It's the country's premier beach destination and a major fishing port.

## Key Attractions

### Beaches
- **Cox's Bazar Beach** — 120 km longest unbroken natural sea beach
- **Laboni Beach** — Main beach near town, most popular
- **Himchari Beach** — 18 km south, quieter with waterfall
- **Inani Beach** — 32 km south, coral stones, crystal clear water
- **Teknaf Beach** — Southernmost point, near Myanmar border

### Natural Attractions
- **Himchari National Park** — Hills, waterfalls, wildlife (35 km from town)
- **Dulhazra Safari Park** — Bangladesh's first safari park
- **St. Martin's Island** — Only coral island, day trip by boat (2.5 hrs)
- **Sonadia Island** — Bird sanctuary, mangrove forests
- **Maheshkhali Island** — Adinath Temple, Buddhist viharas, salt fields

### Cultural Sites
- **Aggameda Khyang** — Buddhist monastery with ancient manuscripts
- **Ramu** — Buddhist village with 13-foot bronze Buddha statue
- **Cox's Bazar Buddhist Temple** — Hilltop temple with sea views

## Transportation
- **Cox's Bazar Airport (CXB)** — Domestic flights from Dhaka, Chittagong
- **Chittagong Road** — 4-5 hour drive from Chittagong
- **Direct buses** — Dhaka to Cox's Bazar (10-12 hours)
- **Local** — CNG, rickshaw, motorbike taxi, battery-run auto

## Accommodation Areas
- **Hotel Motel Zone (Laboni Point)** — Concentration of hotels, beachfront
- **Kolatoli Road** — Mid-range to luxury hotels
- **Sugandha Beach Area** — Quieter, resort-style
- **Inani/Teknaf** — Eco-resorts, away from crowds
- **St. Martin's Island** — Basic guesthouses only

## Food Specialties
- **Fresh Seafood** — Lobster, crab, pomfret, shrimp (daily catch)
- **Mezban** — Traditional spicy beef curry (Chittagong style)
- **Shutki** — Dried fish preparations
- **Coconut water** — Fresh from beach vendors
- **Beach snacks** — Jhalmuri, fuchka, grilled corn

## Best Time to Visit
November to March (peak season, dry, pleasant). April-May (hot but fewer crowds). Avoid June-October (monsoon, cyclones).

## Language
Bengali (Chittagonian dialect), English (tourist areas)

## Safety Tips
- Swim only in designated areas with lifeguards
- Beware of strong currents and rip tides
- Check cyclone warnings before travel
- Secure valuables on beach
- Use registered boat operators for island trips
