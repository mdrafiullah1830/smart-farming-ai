# Sylhet District

## Overview
Sylhet is a metropolitan city in northeastern Bangladesh, known for its tea gardens, rolling hills, and spiritual significance. It's the gateway to the Surma Valley and home to the shrine of Hazrat Shah Jalal.

## Key Attractions

### Historical & Spiritual Sites
- **Shah Jalal Dargah** — Shrine of the 14th century Sufi saint Hazrat Shah Jalal
- **Shah Paran Dargah** — Shrine of Shah Jalal's nephew, Hazrat Shah Paran
- **Murari Chand College** — Historic college established 1892, colonial architecture

### Natural Attractions
- **Ratargul Swamp Forest** — Bangladesh's only swamp forest, "Amazon of Bangladesh"
- **Jaflong** — Stone collection area on Dawki River, scenic hills and waterfalls
- **Lalakhal** — Turquoise blue canal surrounded by hills
- **Srimangal Tea Gardens** — "Tea Capital of Bangladesh" with endless tea estates
- **Lawachara National Park** — Tropical rainforest, home to hoolock gibbons
- **Madhabkunda Waterfall** — Largest waterfall in Bangladesh
- **Bisnakandi** — Stone quarry area with crystal clear water

### Cultural Sites
- **Osmani Museum** — Birthplace of General M.A.G. Osmani, Bangladesh's first Commander-in-Chief
- **Keane Bridge** — Historic bridge over Surma River (1936)

## Transportation
- **Osmani International Airport (ZYL)** — Domestic and limited international flights
- **Sylhet Railway Station** — Connected to Dhaka, Chittagong
- **Kadamtali Bus Terminal** — Major bus terminal for inter-district travel
- **Local transport** — CNG auto-rickshaws, rickshaws, ride-sharing (Pathao, Shohoz)

## Accommodation Areas
- **Dargah Gate/Downtown** — Near shrines, budget to mid-range
- **VIP Road/Amberkhana** — Upscale area, better hotels
- **Srimangal** — Tea garden resorts, eco-lodges
- **Jaflong/Lalakhal** — Riverside resorts

## Food Specialties
- **Panta Ilish** — Fermented rice with hilsa fish (traditional breakfast)
- **Shatkora Beef** — Beef cooked with wild citrus (shatkora)
- **Seven-layer Tea** — Famous layered tea from Srimangal
- **Akni** — Sylheti style biryani
- **Nuni Puri** — Fried bread with dal
- **Citrus fruits** — Shatkora, hatkora, ada zamir

## Best Time to Visit
October to March (pleasant weather). Monsoon (June-September) for lush tea gardens and waterfalls.

## Estimated Costs (per day, per person)
- Budget: ৳1,500-3,000
- Mid-range: ৳4,000-8,000
- Luxury: ৳12,000+

## Language
Sylheti dialect (primary), Standard Bengali, English (tourist areas)

## Safety Tips
- Hire local guide for forest/waterfall visits
- Check weather before hill trips (landslides in monsoon)
- Respect religious customs at dargahs
- Carry cash for remote areas
