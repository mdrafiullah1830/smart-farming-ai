# Khulna District

## Overview
Khulna is the third-largest city in Bangladesh, located in the southwestern region. It's the gateway to the Sundarbans (world's largest mangrove forest) and an industrial hub with jute, newsprint, and shipbuilding.

## Key Attractions

### Natural Attractions
- **Sundarbans Mangrove Forest** — UNESCO World Heritage, Royal Bengal Tiger habitat (access via Khulna/Mongla)
- **Sundarbans National Park** — Wildlife sanctuary, boat safaris
- **Karamjal Wildlife Center** — Deer, crocodile breeding center in Sundarbans
- **Hiron Point (Nilkamal)** — Tiger spotting, watchtower
- **Katka** — Beach, deer, bird watching in Sundarbans

### Historical & Cultural
- **Khan Jahan Ali's Tomb** — 15th century saint's mausoleum in Bagerhat (nearby)
- **Shait Gumbad Mosque (Sixty Dome Mosque)** — UNESCO site, 1459, Bagerhat
- **Nine Dome Mosque** — Bagerhat, terracotta decoration
- **Ronobijoypur Mosque** — Largest single-dome mosque in Bangladesh
- **Khulna Divisional Museum** — Archaeological and ethnographic collections

### Industrial
- **Khulna Shipyard** — Largest shipbuilding yard
- **Daulatpur Industrial Area** — Jute mills, textile factories
- **Mongla Port** — Second seaport of Bangladesh

## Transportation
- **Jessore Airport (JSR)** — Nearest airport (45 min drive), domestic flights
- **Khulna Railway Station** — Terminus for Khulna-Dhaka line
- **Bus terminals** — Sonadanga, Phultala (inter-district)
- **Water transport** — Launch/steamer to Sundarbans, Mongla
- **Local** — Rickshaw, CNG, battery auto, city bus

## Accommodation Areas
- **Sir Iqbal Road/KDA Avenue** — Main commercial, mid-range hotels
- **Boyra/Gallamari** — Residential, quieter options
- **Mongla** — Port area, gateway to Sundarbans
- **Sundarbans** — Eco-lodges, forest rest houses (book via Forest Dept)

## Food Specialties
- **Chingri Malai Curry** — Prawn in coconut milk
- **Shutki Maach** — Dried fish preparations (shutki bhorta)
- **Khulna Biryani** — Distinct style
- **Coconut sweets** — Narikel naru, coconut cakes
- **River fish** — Rui, Katla, Boal from Padma/Rupsha

## Best Time to Visit
November to February (best for Sundarbans). March-May (hot). June-October (monsoon, limited Sundarbans access).

## Safety Tips
- **Sundarbans**: Only with licensed guide/operator, Forest Dept permit required
- Tiger encounters rare but follow guide instructions strictly
- Carry medicines, insect repellent for forest trips
- Check weather/cyclone warnings before coastal travel
