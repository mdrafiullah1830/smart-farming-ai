# Mymensingh District

## Overview
Mymensingh is a historic city in north-central Bangladesh, located on the banks of the Brahmaputra River. Known for its educational institutions, cultural heritage, and the famous Shashi Lodge and Alexander Castle.

## Key Attractions

### Historical Sites
- **Shashi Lodge (Alexander Castle)** — 19th century palace, now Teacher's Training College
- **Bangladesh Agricultural University (BAU)** — 1961, largest agricultural university, beautiful campus
- **Mymensingh Museum** — Archaeological artifacts, folk art, zamindar collections
- **Gauripur Zamindar Palace** — 18th century palace in nearby Gauripur
- **Muktagacha Zamindar Palace** — 300-year-old palace complex with temples

### Natural Attractions
- **Brahmaputra River** — Riverside walk, boat rides, sunset views
- **Bhaluka Pilot Project** — Agricultural research, mango orchards
- **Madhupur National Park** — Sal forest, wildlife (50 km), tribal villages
- **Ramsagar (Mymensingh)** — Large pond, recreational area

### Cultural & Educational
- **Ananda Mohan College** — 1908, heritage buildings
- **Mymensingh Girls' Cadet College** — First girls' cadet college
- **Shambhuganj Bridge** — Iconic bridge over Brahmaputra
- **Bangabandhu Sheikh Mujibur Rahman Agricultural University** — Gazipur (nearby)

## Transportation
- **Mymensingh Railway Station** — Major station on Dhaka-Mymensingh line (2.5-3 hrs from Dhaka)
- **Bus** — Direct from Dhaka (Mohakhali, 2.5-3 hrs via N3)
- **Local** — Rickshaw, CNG, battery auto, city bus, river boat

## Accommodation Areas
- **Choto Bazar/Notun Bazar** — City center, budget to mid-range
- **BAU Campus area** — Guest houses, quieter
- **Bridge area (Shambhuganj)** — Near river, developing hotels
- **Muktagacha/Gauripur** — Heritage homestays

## Food Specialties
- **Muktagacha Monda** — Famous sweet (milk-based, GI tagged)
- **Brahmaputra River Fish** — Rui, Katla, Mrigel, Boal
- **Kachchi Biryani** — Mymensingh style
- **Pitha** — Winter specialties
- **Jackfruit** — Seasonal (summer), many preparations

## Best Time to Visit
November to February (pleasant). March-May (hot, jackfruit season). June-October (monsoon, river full).

## Estimated Costs (per day, per person)

## Language
Bengali, English (educational hub)

## Safety Tips
- River currents strong in monsoon
- BAU campus requires permission for entry
- Carry cash for local markets
- Summer afternoons very hot
