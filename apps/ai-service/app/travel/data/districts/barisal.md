# Barisal District

## Overview
Barisal (officially Barishal) is a major city in southern Bangladesh, known as the "Venice of Bengal" for its numerous rivers, canals, and waterways. It's the gateway to the coastal districts and Kuakata sea beach.

## Key Attractions

### Natural Attractions
- **Kuakata Sea Beach** — "Daughter of the Sea", panoramic sunrise AND sunset over Bay of Bengal (70 km)
- **Durga Sagar** — Large pond with temple, migratory birds (winter)
- **Lebur Char** — River island, sandy beach, fishing community
- **Nalchira Canal** — Scenic waterway, boat rides
- **Ratargul Swamp Forest** — Partly in Barisal division (also Sylhet)

### Historical & Cultural
- **Guthia Mosque** — Baitul Aman Jame Masjid, largest mosque complex in Bangladesh (2003)
- **Oxford Mission Church** — 1903 Anglican church, red brick architecture
- **Barisal Divisional Museum** — Archaeology, coins, manuscripts
- **Sher-e-Bangla Memorial Museum** — A.K. Fazlul Huq's ancestral home
- **Ashram of Mahatma Gandhi** — Gandhi stayed here in 1921

### River Experiences
- **Launch/Steamer Journey** — Overnight Dhaka-Barisal launch (classic experience)
- **River Cruise** — Kirtonkhola, Kirtankhola, Arial Khan rivers
- **Floating Market** — Guava floating market (seasonal, July-August)

## Transportation
- **Barisal Airport (BZL)** — Domestic flights from Dhaka
- **Barisal Railway Station** — Newly connected via Padma Bridge rail link
- **Launch/Steamer** — Sadarghat, Dhaka to Barisal (overnight, 8-10 hrs)
- **Bus** — Via Padma Bridge (4-5 hours from Dhaka)
- **Local** — Rickshaw, CNG, boat/launch for inter-river travel

## Accommodation Areas
- **Nathullabad/Kashipur** — City center, mid-range hotels
- **Band Road** — Near launch terminal, budget options
- **Kuakata** — Beach hotels, resorts, cottages
- **Homestays** — Riverbank villages, cultural immersion

## Food Specialties
- **Hilsa Fish (Ilish)** — "Queen of Fish", multiple preparations
- **Chingri Posto** — Prawn with poppy seeds
- **Coconut-based curries** — Fish, vegetable
- **Panta Bhat with fried hilsa** — Pohela Boishakh tradition
- **Guava** — Famous Barisal guavas (seasonal)
- **Sweetmeats** — Chomchom, roshogolla from local shops

## Best Time to Visit
November to March (pleasant). April-May (hot, guava season). June-October (monsoon, rivers full).

## Estimated Costs (per day, per person)
- Budget: ৳1,200-2,500
- Mid-range: ৳3,500-7,000
- Luxury: ৳12,000+ (Kuakata resorts)

## Language
Bengali (Barisali dialect), English

## Safety Tips
- Launch travel: Book reputable operators, check safety equipment
- Monsoon: River erosion, flooding possible
- Kuakata: Swim only in designated zones
- Carry cash for remote riverine areas
