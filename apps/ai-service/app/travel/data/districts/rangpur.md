# Rangpur District

## Overview
Rangpur is a major city in northwestern Bangladesh, known for its historical heritage, agricultural richness, and the famous Tajhat Palace. It's the center of the Rangpur division.

## Key Attractions

### Historical Sites
- **Tajhat Palace (Tajhat Rajbari)** — Early 20th century palace, now a museum with terracotta artifacts
- **Kellaband Mosque** — 16th century terracotta mosque
- **Pirgachha Rajbari** — Zamindar palace with Corinthian columns
- **Devi Chaudhurani Mandir** — Temple associated with legendary queen
- **Vinno Jogot** — Theme park with replicas of world wonders

### Natural & Cultural
- **Ramsagar National Park** — Large man-made lake (1750s), picnic spot, migratory birds
- **Karatoya River** — Ancient river, historical significance
- **Rangpur Zoo** — One of oldest zoos in Bangladesh
- **Carmichael College** — 1916, beautiful colonial campus
- **RDRS Bangladesh** — Development organization, cultural center

### Nearby (Division)
- **Kantaji Temple (Dinajpur)** — 18th century terracotta masterpiece (UNESCO tentative)
- **Nayabad Mosque (Dinajpur)** — Near Kantaji, Mughal architecture
- **Ramsagar (Dinajpur)** — Largest man-made lake in Bangladesh
- **Shopnopuri (Pabna)** — Amusement park, artificial waterfalls

## Transportation
- **Saidpur Airport (SPD)** — Nearest airport (20 km), domestic flights
- **Rangpur Railway Station** — On Dhaka-Rangpur line
- **Bus terminals** — New Bus Terminal, Shyampur (inter-district)
- **Local** — Rickshaw, CNG, battery auto, city bus

## Accommodation Areas
- **Station Road/Station Para** — Near rail station, budget to mid-range
- **Jahaj Company Mor** — Commercial area, hotels
- **Tajhat/Museum area** — Quieter, near palace
- **Saidpur** — Airport vicinity, transit hotels

## Food Specialties
- **Rangpur Lime (Kagzi Lebu)** — Famous citrus, used in cooking
- **Shatkora Beef** — Also popular here (like Sylhet)
- **Misti Doi** — Sweet yogurt
- **Pitha varieties** — Winter rice cakes (bhapa, patishapta, puli)
- **River fish** — From Teesta, Karatoya rivers

## Best Time to Visit
November to February (cool, dry, best for sightseeing). March-May (hot). June-October (monsoon).

## Language
Bengali (Rangpuri dialect), English

## Safety Tips
- Summer heat intense (can exceed 40°C)
- Winter mornings foggy, affect transport
- Carry warm clothes for winter nights
- Respect local customs at religious sites
