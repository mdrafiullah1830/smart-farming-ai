# Rajshahi District

## Overview
Rajshahi is a metropolitan city in northwestern Bangladesh, known as the "Silk City" and "Education City." Famous for mangoes, lychees, silk, and the ancient Puthia temple complex.

## Key Attractions

### Historical Sites
- **Puthia Temple Complex** — Largest cluster of historic Hindu temples in Bangladesh (16th-19th century)
- **Bagha Mosque** — 1523 terracotta mosque, UNESCO tentative list
- **Kusumba Mosque** — 1558 stone mosque with intricate carvings
- **Varendra Research Museum** — Oldest museum in Bangladesh (1910), archaeological artifacts
- **Mahasthangarh** — Ancient urban archaeological site (3rd century BCE), 18 km north

### Natural Attractions
- **Padma River** — Mighty river, chars (river islands), boat rides
- **Barendra Ecology Park** — Biodiversity park
- **Mango Orchards** — Seasonal (May-July), famous for "Gopalbhog", "Himsagar", "Langra"

### Educational & Cultural
- **University of Rajshahi** — Large campus, Shahid Smriti Sangrahashala
- **Rajshahi College** — One of oldest colleges (1873)
- **Silk Factory** — Rajshahi Silk (Borendra Silk), weaving demonstrations

## Transportation
- **Shah Makhdum Airport (RJH)** — Domestic flights from Dhaka
- **Rajshahi Railway Station** — On Dhaka-Rajshahi line
- **Bus terminals** — Kadirganj, New Bus Terminal (inter-district)
- **Local** — Rickshaw, CNG, battery auto, city bus

## Accommodation Areas
- **Shaheb Bazar/Station Road** — Commercial center, budget to mid-range
- **Laalkuthi/Ghoramara** — Residential, quieter hotels
- **Near University** — Guest houses, budget options
- **Puthia** — Heritage homestays near temples

## Food Specialties
- **Mangoes** — World-famous varieties (seasonal)
- **Lychees** — Summer fruit
- **Kachchi Biryani** — Rajshahi style
- **Panta Bhat with Ilish** — Traditional breakfast
- **Silk worms delicacy** — Local specialty (seasonal)

## Best Time to Visit
October to March (pleasant). April-June (mango season, hot). Avoid monsoon.

## Estimated Costs (per day, per person)
- Budget: ৳1,200-2,500
- Mid-range: ৳3,000-6,000
- Luxury: ৳10,000+

## Language
Bengali (Varendri dialect), English

## Safety Tips
- Summer heat can be extreme (40°C+)
- Carry water and sun protection
- Respect religious sites dress codes
