# Chittagong District

## Overview
Chittagong (officially Chattogram) is Bangladesh's second-largest city and principal seaport. Located on the banks of Karnaphuli River, it's the commercial capital with a unique blend of hills, sea, and urban life.

## Key Attractions

### Historical Sites
- **Ethnological Museum** — Only ethnological museum in Bangladesh
- **Zia Memorial Museum** — Circuit House where President Ziaur Rahman was assassinated
- **Chittagong War Cemetery** — Commonwealth war graves (WWII)
- **Portuguese Fort (Firingi Bazar)** — 16th century Portuguese settlement ruins

### Natural Attractions
- **Patenga Beach** — Popular beach at Karnaphuli River mouth
- **Foy's Lake** — Man-made lake with amusement park and resort
- **Batali Hill** — Highest hill in city, panoramic views
- **Karnaphuli River** — River cruises, ship-breaking yards view
- **Sitakunda Eco Park** — Hills, waterfalls, Chandranath Temple

### Cultural & Religious
- **Bayazid Bostami Shrine** — Sufi shrine with sacred turtle pond
- **Anderkilla Shahi Jame Mosque** — 1667 Mughal mosque
- **Chandranath Temple** — Hindu temple on Chandranath Hill (300m climb)
- **Buddhist Monasteries** — Several in Patiya and Rangunia

## Transportation
- **Shah Amanat International Airport (CGP)** — Domestic and international
- **Chittagong Railway Station** — Major rail hub
- **BRTC Bus Terminals** — Dampara, Bahaddarhat for inter-district
- **Water transport** — Sadarghat launch terminal to coastal areas
- **Local** — CNG, rickshaw, city bus, ride-sharing

## Accommodation Areas
- **Agrabad** — Commercial area, business hotels
- **GEC Circle/Khulshi** — Residential, mid-range hotels
- **Patenga** — Beachside hotels and resorts
- **CDA Avenue** — Upscale area, newer hotels
- **Station Road** — Budget options near rail station

## Food Specialties
- **Mezbani Goru** — Spicy beef curry (Chittagong signature dish)
- **Kala Bhuna** — Dark beef bhuna
- **Chittagong Biryani** — Distinct from Dhaka style
- **Seafood** — Fresh fish, prawn, crab from harbor
- **Pitha** — Traditional rice cakes (winter)

## Best Time to Visit
November to February (cool, dry). March-May (hot). June-October (monsoon).

Bengali (Chittagonian dialect), English (business/tourism)

## Safety Tips
- Avoid ship-breaking yard areas (hazardous)
- Use registered taxis/ride-sharing at night
- Stay hydrated in summer heat
- Respect port security zones
