# Ahsan Manzil (Pink Palace)

## Overview
Ahsan Manzil, known as the "Pink Palace," was the official residential palace and seat of the Nawabs of Dhaka. Located on the banks of the Buriganga River in Old Dhaka, it's one of the most significant architectural monuments of Bangladesh.

## History
- **Original construction**: 1859-1872 by Nawab Abdul Ghani
- **Renamed**: After his son Nawab Khwaja Ahsanullah
- **Reconstruction**: 1888 after tornado damage, completed 1892
- **Peak period**: Late 19th - early 20th century, center of Dhaka's social/political life
- **Decline**: After 1952 (abolition of zamindari system), fell into disrepair
- **Restoration**: 1985-1992 by Government of Bangladesh
- **Museum opening**: 1992 as National Museum property

## Architecture
- **Style**: Indo-Saracenic Revival (Mughal + European)
- **Distinctive feature**: Pink color (originally white, painted pink during restoration)
- **Layout**: Two-storied, 125 rooms, wide verandahs, central dome
- **Ground floor**: Administrative offices, guest rooms, storage
- **First floor**: Residential quarters, Durbar Hall, library, billiard room
- **Dome**: 27.13m high, octagonal, visible from river
- **Materials**: Brick, lime mortar, cast iron, marble, chandeliers

## Key Rooms & Features
1. **Durbar Hall** — Grand audience hall, crystal chandeliers, gilt mirrors
2. **Drawing Room** — European furniture, portraits of Nawabs
3. **Library** — Rare manuscripts, books in Persian, Arabic, Urdu, English
4. **Billiard Room** — Original billiard table
5. **Dining Room** — Silver dining set, ceramic collection
6. **Bedrooms** — Period furniture, personal effects
7. **Verandahs** — River-facing, cast iron railings, cooling breeze

## Visiting Information
- **Location**: Islampur, Old Dhaka (Kumartoli)
- **Hours**: Saturday-Wednesday 10:30 AM - 5:30 PM; Friday 3:00 PM - 7:30 PM
- **Closed**: Thursday, government holidays
- **Entry Fee**: Bangladeshi ৳20, SAARC ৳100, Foreign ৳200

- **From Gulshan/Banani**: 45-60 min

## Cultural Significance
- Center of Muslim aristocracy in Eastern Bengal
- Hosted British Viceroys, Indian leaders
- All India Muslim League founded here (1906)
- Cultural hub: music, poetry, literature patronage
- Symbol of Dhaka's Nawabi heritage

## Best Time to Visit
October-March. Late afternoon for river view and golden hour photography.
