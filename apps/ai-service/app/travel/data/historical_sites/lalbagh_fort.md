# Lalbagh Fort

## Overview
Lalbagh Fort (Fort Aurangabad) is an incomplete 17th century Mughal fort complex in Old Dhaka, started by Prince Muhammad Azam in 1678 and later continued by Shaista Khan. It's one of the most iconic historical landmarks of Dhaka.

## History
- **Construction started**: 1678 by Prince Muhammad Azam (son of Emperor Aurangzeb)
- **Continued by**: Shaista Khan (Subahdar of Bengal) after 1679
- **Abandoned**: After death of Shaista Khan's daughter Pari Bibi in 1684
- **Status**: Never completed; only three main structures built

## Architecture
- **Area**: 18 acres (originally larger, now reduced)
- **Style**: Mughal architecture with Bengali influences
- **Materials**: Red bricks, plaster, marble, glazed tiles
- **Layout**: Fortified enclosure with gates, mosque, tomb, audience hall

## Key Structures
1. **Diwan-i-Aam (Audience Hall)** — Two-storied, used for public audience, now museum
2. **Mosque** — Three-domed, rectangular, beautiful terracotta ornamentation
3. **Tomb of Pari Bibi** — Central chamber with marble cenotaph, eight surrounding rooms, exquisite pietra dura work
4. **Hammam (Bathhouse)** — Adjacent to Diwan-i-Aam, advanced water system
5. **South Gate** — Main entrance, three-storied, guard rooms

## Museum Collection
- Mughal miniature paintings
- Coins (Mughal, British era)
- Weapons (swords, daggers, guns)
- Pottery and ceramics
- Calligraphy specimens
- Farmans (royal decrees)

## Visiting Information
- **Location**: Lalbagh, Old Dhaka
- **Hours**: Saturday-Wednesday 9:00 AM - 5:00 PM (summer), 9:00 AM - 4:30 PM (winter); Friday 2:30 PM - 5:00 PM/4:30 PM
- **Closed**: Thursday, government holidays
- **Entry Fee**: Bangladeshi ৳20, SAARC ৳100, Foreign ৳200
- **Photography**: Allowed (no flash inside museum)

## How to Reach
- **From Gulshan/Banani**: 45-60 min by car/CNG
- **From Airport**: 45 min via Airport Road
- **Public Transport**: Bus to Gulistan, then rickshaw/CNG to Lalbagh
- **Ride-sharing**: Uber/Pathao to "Lalbagh Fort"

## Nearby Attractions
- Ahsan Manzil (15 min)
- Star Mosque (10 min)
- Armenian Church (10 min)
- Sadarghat River Terminal (15 min)
- Shankhari Bazar (Hindu street, 10 min)

## Cultural Significance
- Symbol of Mughal presence in Bengal
- Site of 1857 Sepoy Mutiny planning in Dhaka
- Cultural events, poetry readings in fort grounds
- Popular photography location

## Best Time to Visit
October-March (pleasant weather). Early morning for photography, avoid Friday crowds.

## Tips
- Wear comfortable shoes (walking on uneven surfaces)
- Carry water, limited facilities inside
- Hire guide at entrance for detailed history
- Combine with Old Dhaka walking tour
- Respect mosque prayer times
