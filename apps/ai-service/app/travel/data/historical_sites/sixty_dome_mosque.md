# Sixty Dome Mosque (Shait Gumbad Mosque)

## Overview
The Sixty Dome Mosque (Shait Gumbad Masjid) in Bagerhat is the largest Sultanate-era mosque in Bangladesh and a UNESCO World Heritage Site (1985). Built in 1459 by Khan Jahan Ali, it represents the pinnacle of Khan Jahan style architecture.

## History
- **Builder**: Khan Jahan Ali (Turkish saint-warrior, governor of Sundarbans)
- **Date**: 1459 CE (863 AH)
- **Context**: Part of Khalifatabad (mint town) founded by Khan Jahan Ali
- **Restoration**: 1903-04 (British), 1980s (Government), ongoing conservation
- **UNESCO**: Inscribed 1985 as part of "Historic Mosque City of Bagerhat"

## Architecture
- **Style**: Khan Jahan style (Tuğluk-derived, local adaptations)
- **Dimensions**: 49m × 33m (external), 38m × 23m (internal)
- **Domes**: 60 domes (7 rows × 7 columns = 49 + 11 corner/entrance) — actually 77 domes total
- **Pillars**: 60 stone pillars (11 rows × 6 columns minus entrance space)
- **Arches**: 11 arched openings on east, 7 each on north/south
- **Materials**: Brick (locally made), stone pillars (basalt from Rajmahal Hills)
- **Wall thickness**: 2.4m (unusually thick for climate control)

## Key Features
1. **Prayer Hall** — Hypostyle (forest of pillars), 7 longitudinal aisles, 11 transverse aisles
2. **Mihrabs** — 11 mihrabs (prayer niches), central one largest with terracotta decoration
3. **Mimbar** — Stone pulpit (rare in Bengal mosques)
4. **Corner Towers** — Four massive circular towers, spiral staircases to roof
5. **Entrance** — Monumental eastern gateway with recessed arches
6. **Terracotta Ornamentation** — Geometric, floral, arabesque on mihrabs, arches, cornices
7. **Ventilation** — Clerestory windows, dome openings for airflow
8. **Acoustics** — Designed for voice projection without amplification

## Khan Jahan Ali Complex (Nearby)
- **Khan Jahan Ali's Tomb** — Single-domed, stone sarcophagus, pilgrimage site
- **Nine Dome Mosque** — 15th century, beautiful terracotta
- **Ronobijoypur Mosque** — Largest single-dome mosque in Bangladesh
- **Bibi Begni Mosque** — Unique curved cornice
- **Ghora Dighi** — Large tank excavated by Khan Jahan Ali

## Visiting Information
- **Location**: Bagerhat Sadar, Bagerhat District (Khulna Division)
- **Hours**: Saturday-Wednesday 9:00 AM - 5:00 PM (summer), 9:00 AM - 4:30 PM (winter); Friday 2:30 PM - 5:00 PM/4:30 PM
- **Closed**: Thursday, government holidays
- **Entry Fee**: Bangladeshi ৳20, SAARC ৳100, Foreign ৳200
- **Photography**: Allowed

## How to Reach
- **From Khulna**: 45 min by road (35 km)
- **From Dhaka**: 6-7 hours by bus (direct to Bagerhat) or launch + road
- **From Mongla**: 30 min (gateway to Sundarbans)
- **Public**: Khulna-Bagerhat bus, then rickshaw/CNG
- **Railway**: Nearest - Khulna (then road)

## Nearby Attractions

- Mongla Port (30 km)

## Cultural Significance
- **Architectural mastery** — Largest, most sophisticated Sultanate mosque in Bengal
- **Khan Jahan legacy** — Saint, administrator, city builder, environmentalist (planted trees, dug tanks)
- **Living heritage** — Still used for prayers, annual urs (death anniversary) draws thousands
- **World Heritage** — Part of "Mosque City of Bagerhat" (50+ monuments)

## Best Time to Visit
November-February (cool, dry, best for Sundarbans combination). Early morning for photography.

## Tips
- Combine with Sundarbans trip (Mongla base)
- Friday prayers — visit before 12 PM or after 2 PM
- Women: Headscarf required inside prayer hall
- Remove shoes at entrance
- Hire local guide for architectural details
- Visit Khan Jahan Ali tomb nearby
- Carry water, limited facilities
