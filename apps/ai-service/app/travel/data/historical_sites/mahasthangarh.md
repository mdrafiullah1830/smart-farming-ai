# Mahasthangarh

## Overview
Mahasthangarh (Pundranagara) in Bogura is the earliest urban archaeological site in Bangladesh, dating to 3rd century BCE. It was the ancient capital of Pundravardhana and a continuous settlement for 1,500+ years through Maurya, Gupta, Pala, Sena, and Sultanate periods.

## History
- **Earliest occupation**: 3rd century BCE (Northern Black Polished Ware)
- **Capital of**: Pundravardhana (Maurya, Gupta, Pala, Sena periods)
- **Key periods**: 
  - Maurya (3rd century BCE) — provincial capital
  - Gupta (4th-6th century) — flourishing urban center
  - Pala (8th-12th century) — Buddhist center, monasteries
  - Sena (11th-12th century) — Hindu revival
  - Sultanate (13th-16th century) — Islamic structures
  - Mughal (16th-18th century) — decline, shift to Bogura
- **Rediscovery**: 1808 (Buchanan Hamilton), systematic excavation 1928-29 (K.N. Dikshit)
- **Continuous excavation**: Since 1990s (French-Bangladesh joint mission)

## Architecture & Layout
- **Citadel**: Fortified rectangle, 1.5km × 1.4km, 6m high ramparts
- **Area**: ~185 hectares (citadel + suburbs)
- **Defenses**: Rampart (mud brick core, baked brick facing), moat (Karna River as natural moat)
- **Gates**: 4 main gates (north, south, east, west) with guard rooms
- **Internal layout**: Grid pattern streets, residential blocks, public buildings

## Key Structures (Inside Citadel)
1. **Govinda Bhita Temple** — 3rd-15th century, multiple rebuilding phases, terracotta plaques
2. **Khodar Pathar Bhita** — Stone slab structure, possibly Gupta period altar
3. **Mankalir Dhap** — Monastery complex, Pala period
4. **Parasuramer Prasad** — Palace mound, Sena period
5. **Jiat Kunda** — Well with legendary healing properties
6. **Bairagir Bhita** — Buddhist stupa base
7. **Islamic structures** — Mosques, tombs (Sultanate/Mughal) over earlier ruins

## Suburban Sites (Outside Citadel)
- **Godai Bari Dhap** — Monastery, 8th-11th century
- **Totaram Panditer Dhap** — Vihara, inscribed seal found
- **Narapatir Dhap** — Palace mound
- **Gokul Medh (Lakshindarer Medh)** — 3km south, terraced temple (7th century)
- **Vasu Vihara** — 10km west, major monastery (Xuanzang visited)

## Museum (Mahasthangarh Site Museum)
- **Established**: 1967
- **Collection**: Terracotta plaques, beads, coins (punch-marked, Kushan, Gupta, Pala, Sultanate), seals, inscriptions, pottery, stone sculptures, bronze images
- **Highlights**: Brahmi inscription (3rd century BCE), Gupta gold coins, Pala bronze Buddha

## Visiting Information
- **Location**: Shibganj Upazila, Bogura District (Rajshahi Division)
- **Hours**: Saturday-Wednesday 9:00 AM - 5:00 PM (summer), 9:00 AM - 4:30 PM (winter); Friday 2:30 PM - 5:00 PM/4:30 PM
- **Closed**: Thursday, government holidays
- **Entry Fee**: Bangladeshi ৳20, SAARC ৳100, Foreign ৳200 (site + museum)
- **Photography**: Allowed

## How to Reach
- **From Dhaka**: 4-5 hours (200 km via Bangabandhu Bridge)
- **From Rajshahi**: 2 hours (80 km)
- **From Bogura town**: 12 km (20 min by CNG/auto)
- **Public**: Dhaka-Bogura bus, then local transport
- **Railway**: Bogura station (then 20 min road)

## Nearby Attractions
- **Gokul Medh** (3 km) — Terraced temple
- **Bhimer Jangal** — Ancient embankment
- **Bogura town** — Mahasthan Garh Museum, local food
- **Paharpur** (60 km) — Somapura Mahavihara (combinable day trip)
- **Vasu Vihara** (10 km west)

## Cultural Significance
- **Earliest city** — Oldest urban site in Bangladesh
- **Continuous occupation** — 1,800+ years of layered history
- **Xuanzang visit** — Chinese pilgrim (638 CE) described 20 monasteries, 100+ monks
- **Religious syncretism** — Buddhist, Hindu, Islamic layers
- **Trade center** — On ancient trade routes (river + land)

## Best Time to Visit
November-February (cool, dry, best visibility). October-November for photography.

## Tips
- Large site — hire CNG/auto for internal transport or walk 3-4 hours
- Hire guide from museum for interpretation
- Morning light best for rampart photography
- Carry water, hat, sunscreen (minimal shade)
- Combine with Gokul Medh and Paharpur
- Museum has AC, good for midday break
- Respect local worship at Jiat Kunda, Mazar areas
