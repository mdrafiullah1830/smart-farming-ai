Rice ×Leaf problem ×
🎙 

✨ Answer

🛡 High confidence · 92%

Your rice leaves are likely affected by nitrogen (N) deficiency.

Yellowing leaves, especially older lower leaves, usually indicate a lack of nitrogen. This reduces chlorophyll and makes the plant look pale. It is a common problem during the tillering stage.

🌿 Action Plan

1Apply urea fertilizer
Use 70–100 kg urea per acre in split doses. Apply when soil is moist.
2Ensure proper water management
Maintain 2–5 cm standing water in the field.
3Check other possible causes
Rule out zinc deficiency or root damage.
4Monitor and follow up
Observe new leaf color after 7–10 days.
